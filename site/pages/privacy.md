# Privacy policy

_Last updated October 8, 2026._

wr1t3r is a notebook app that runs in your browser at my.wr1t3r.app. I make it on my own, and this page covers everything the app sees and where it goes. The short version: your notes stay between your browser and the storage you pick, I have no server that keeps them, and I never sell, share or look at your data.

## Your notes

- Your notes and pictures go only between your browser and the storage you sign in with: your Dropbox or your Google Drive. I have no server that stores, reads or copies them.
- A copy is kept in your browser so the app works offline. Signing out on a device removes it there.
- **Dropbox** access is limited to wr1t3r's own app folder (`Apps/wr1t3r`). You can remove it any time in your Dropbox settings.

## Google user data

If you keep your notes in Google Drive or connect Google Calendar, wr1t3r asks Google for these permissions, and only these:

- **Google Drive, files wr1t3r made** (`drive.file`): to save your notes and pictures as files in a folder called "wr1t3r" in your Drive, and read them back. wr1t3r can see only the files it created or that you put in through its Upload button. It can't see anything else in your Drive.
- **Google Calendar events** (`calendar.events`): to show your events in the agenda, month view and Timeline; to add the events you create in wr1t3r, or import from a calendar file you choose; and, if you turn on reminders through Google Calendar, to add a short event for each upcoming task reminder or medicine dose and delete it once it's done or moved.
- **Your calendar list** (`calendar.calendarlist.readonly`): so you can pick which calendars the agenda shows and which one reminders go to.
- **Your email address** (`openid`, `email`): only to show which Google account is connected, in Settings.

How that data is handled:

- **Where it goes.** Your files, events and calendar list travel straight between your browser and Google. They never pass through a server of mine.
- **Sign-in.** Google gives a lasting sign-in only to an app that holds a secret key, so wr1t3r's small server adds that key when your browser swaps Google's sign-in code for access, and when it renews that access. Your sign-in passes through on its way back to your browser and isn't stored or logged. The sign-in itself is kept in your browser on that device.
- **What I store.** Nothing. I have no database of users, files, events or email addresses.
- **Sharing.** I don't sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, and wr1t3r doesn't show ads or use any analytics. It isn't used to build profiles, for advertising, or to train AI or machine learning models.
- **Humans.** I can't read your Google data, because it never reaches me. If you send me something yourself to help with a problem, I use it only for that and delete it afterward.
- **Removing access.** In wr1t3r, **Settings > Features > Disconnect Google Calendar** (or signing out of a Drive notebook) tells Google to cancel wr1t3r's access and deletes the sign-in from that browser. You can also remove wr1t3r any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Your notes and events stay in your Drive and Calendar, where you can delete them yourself.

wr1t3r's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Other things the app sends

- **License checks** send your license key and a device name (like "wr1t3r on iPhone") to Polar, the store that sells it, about once a week. Your purchase details (name, email) are held by Polar and used only for your license and receipts.
- **Lookups you ask for** (a book or film title, a word's definition) send that search text to the public service that answers it, such as Open Library or Wikidata.
- **Publishing**, if you use it, sends the notes you choose to your own GitHub repo. The GitHub token you paste stays in your browser.
- No analytics, no ads, no tracking cookies.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date at the top. If a change would let wr1t3r use Google data in a new way, the app asks for your permission again first.
