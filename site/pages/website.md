--- features

## One click to Neocities

Paste your Neocities API key once. After that, Publish to Neocities is right in the globe menu. Only the files that changed are sent, so a new post goes up in seconds. Pages you unpublish come down. Files you put on your site yourself are left alone. If a file isn't allowed on a free site, wr1t3r tells you which one and sends the rest.

Not on Neocities? Publish to GitHub Pages in one click, let Netlify or Cloudflare Pages pick it up from there, or download a .zip for any host.

## Design your front page with blocks

Add a heading, some text, a picture, your latest posts, a list of links or a divider, put them in order, and watch the page change as you go. Show the five newest posts from your journal, the three newest sketches below them, or anything else you like. No HTML needed.

Rather type it? The front page is an ordinary note, so you can write it yourself, and switch between the two whenever you want.

## Follow the sites you love

Neighbors is a reader for the small web, built into wr1t3r. Follow a site by its address and its new posts show up in one list, checked morning and evening while wr1t3r is open. Most blogs have a feed. For a hand-made Neocities site without one, wr1t3r notices when the site changes and tells you.

Bring the list you already have from another feed reader with OPML, and find new sites through the ones your neighbors read.

![Neighbors: the sites you follow on the left, and their newest posts on the right, with a reply to one of your posts at the top](/shots/web-neighbors.webp)

## Reply from your own site

Click Reply on a neighbor's post and wr1t3r starts a new note that answers it. Publish it and it's a post on your site that links back to theirs, and if they follow you in wr1t3r, it shows up for them as a reply.

When someone replies to you, it shows up at the top of Neighbors. Choose to show it and the first few lines appear under your post with a link to the rest on their site. Your own replies to your own posts show there in full.

![A published post with its responses underneath: the start of a neighbor's reply with a link to read the rest on their site, and the author's own follow-up](/shots/web-replies.webp)

## Questions

### Do I need a paid Neocities site?

No. A free site is plenty. Free sites don't take audio or video files, so wr1t3r leaves those out unless you ask for them.

### What happens to the site I already have on Neocities?

wr1t3r's front page replaces your `index.html`, and its pages go alongside your other files. Files you made yourself are never deleted. When you unpublish a note, only the page wr1t3r put there comes down. If you'd rather keep your own front page, publish wr1t3r's pages with your own stylesheet and link to them from it.

### Does wr1t3r keep my Neocities API key?

No. Neocities won't take uploads straight from a web page, so the key passes through wr1t3r's server on its way to Neocities each time you publish, and isn't kept there. If you tick Remember, it's kept on your device only. Make a new key on neocities.org at any time and the old one stops working.

### Can I use my own domain?

Yes. If your Neocities site has its own domain, wr1t3r uses it for your feed and your links.

### Do people need wr1t3r to follow me or reply?

No. Your feed works in any feed reader, and anyone can link to your posts from their own site. wr1t3r makes following and replying a click, and shows replies from sites you follow.

### How do I start?

Turn on Website in Settings › Features, click the globe on a note, and choose Export as website. [Help walks through it](/help.html#neocities-direct).
